House Calendar

A private, touchscreen family calendar that runs on a Raspberry Pi in one household.

House Calendar shows the household's Google Calendar events on a wall-mounted screen, alongside the weather, a chore list and a grocery list. Family members can add and edit events from the screen, and changes sync with Google Calendar.

It is a personal project, not a public service: it is used only by the household that runs it, and nobody else can sign in to it.
