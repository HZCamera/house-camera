Privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

What House Calendar is

House Calendar is a personal family calendar that runs on a Raspberry Pi inside one home. It is not a commercial product or public service, and only members of that household use it.

What Google data it uses

When the household connects a Google account, House Calendar asks for access to Google Calendar so it can:

  • show the household's calendars and their events on the home screen, and
  • add, change or delete events when someone does that on the screen.

It does not access any other Google data, such as email, contacts, files or photos.

Where the data goes

Calendar events are fetched directly from Google and shown on the screen in the home. The Google sign-in token is stored only on the household's own Raspberry Pi. House Calendar has no servers or database of its own, and it does not send calendar data anywhere else.

House Calendar does not sell, share, transfer or use Google data for advertising, and does not use it for any purpose other than showing and editing the household's calendar.

House Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other information

Chores, groceries and display settings entered on the screen are saved only on the household's Raspberry Pi. To show the weather, the screen sends the household's approximate location (the town chosen in Settings) to Open-Meteo, a free weather service.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions. Deleting the House Calendar folder from the Raspberry Pi removes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email address shown on House Calendar's Google sign-in screen.
